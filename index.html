How I lead

  • Activity is not impact.

    Usage counts show exposure, not whether outputs were accurate or decisions improved.

  • A second model is a critic, not a verifier.

    Models share blind spots; agreement between models is not ground truth.

  • Permission to build is not permission to deploy.

    Connecting data, exposing tools, and authorising autonomous action are distinct risk gates.

  • Solve the data problem before adding an agent.

    Deterministic automation or cleaner structure beats fragile autonomy whenever possible.

  • Price total human effort.

    Verification, review, and correction time belong inside the true ROI of every AI workflow.

  • Surface uncertainty explicitly.

    Systems that expose what is unverified earn trust; systems that hide gaps fail silently.

Writing

Short essays on AI measurement, evaluation, and runtime control.

  • Usage is not quality: what to measure when you roll out AI

    Usage counts show who clicked. They do not show whether the work got better, or what it cost to check.

  • A second model is a critic, not a verifier

    Asking one model to review another can improve a draft. It cannot tell you the draft is true.

  • Read freely, write carefully: controls for AI agents that act

    An agent that reads can mislead you. An agent that writes or sends can do something you cannot take back.
