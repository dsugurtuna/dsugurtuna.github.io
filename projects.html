Core AI systems

Four connected architectures built to test how AI systems can scale safely and economically.

  • Control execution

    agent-guardrails

    How do you let an AI agent act without it sending the wrong thing, twice, to the wrong people?

    Runtime control layer for AI agents that write or send: allow/draft/approve modes, execution-time re-validation, idempotency, tamper-evident hash-chained audit logs, and a kill switch.

    • Agentic AI
    • Tool-use guardrails
    • Audit log
    • OWASP LLM

  • Verify quality

    evidence-synthesis-eval

    Does an AI synthesis say what the sources say, what did it miss, and what did verification cost?

    Open evaluation harness built on UK AISI Inspect: faithfulness, importance-weighted coverage, misstatement rates, blinded human-judge agreement (Cohen’s kappa), and checking effort.

    • LLM Evaluation
    • Inspect AI
    • Benchmarking
    • Faithfulness

  • Govern & assure

    ai-assurance-kit

    How can a team run proportionate AI assurance in an afternoon rather than a quarter?

    Policy-as-code assurance engine turning AI use cases into schema-validated YAML records, 26 explainable checks, executive one-page cards, three published GOV.UK ATRS adaptations, and NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001 crosswalks.
